Why We Charge Membership Fees

ORRA is a non-profit organization run by volunteers. Membership fees help us:

  • Provide quality training and development programs
  • Maintain our referee review and appointments systems
  • Cover insurance and administrative costs
  • Fund social events and awards
  • Support referee welfare initiatives
  • Provide resources and equipment where needed

Frequently Asked Questions

When are fees due?

Annual membership fees are typically due at the start of the season. Specific deadline information will be communicated via email and newsletter.

What if I can't afford the fees?

Please reach out to us confidentially. We never want fees to be a barrier to refereeing and will work with you to find a solution.

What happens if I don't pay?

While we encourage all members to pay their subs to support the association, we understand circumstances vary. Please talk to us if you're having difficulty - we're here to support you.

📧 Subscribe to Our Newsletter

Want to receive ORRA newsletters directly to your inbox? All current members are automatically subscribed using their membership email address.

Not receiving newsletters? Please contact the Secretary to update your email address.

New members: You'll be added to the mailing list when you join ORRA.

📮 Submit Content for the Newsletter

Have a story, achievement, or announcement to share? We'd love to feature it in our next newsletter!

Content we're looking for:

  • Match day highlights and memorable moments
  • Referee achievements and milestones
  • Training session recaps
  • Photos from matches and events
  • Tips and advice from experienced referees
  • Upcoming events and opportunities

Submit content to: secretary@orra.co.nz

Deadline: 25th of each month for inclusion in next newsletter
